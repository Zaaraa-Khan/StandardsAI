# StandardsAI

## AI-Powered Indian Standards Recommendation Platform

StandardsAI is a procurement-focused platform designed to help users identify relevant Indian Standards (IS) for products, technical specifications, and tender requirements.

The platform provides a structured interface for entering procurement requirements, analyzing them, viewing recommended standards, checking compliance, and preparing procurement-related reports.

---

## Problem Statement

Procurement teams often need to identify the correct Indian Standards while preparing product specifications and tender documents.

Finding the appropriate standards can be challenging because of:

- Large numbers of Indian Standards
- Overlapping scopes between standards
- Revisions and amendments
- Related and allied standards
- Testing and safety requirements
- Installation and terminology requirements
- Potentially outdated references

StandardsAI aims to simplify this process through a centralized and user-friendly procurement intelligence interface.

---

## Key Features

### Procurement Requirement Input
Users can enter product descriptions, technical specifications, or tender requirements to begin the recommendation process.

### Tender Upload Interface
The platform provides an interface for uploading tender documents for analysis.

### Standards Recommendation
The system presents relevant standards based on the entered procurement requirements.

### Relevance & Confidence
Recommended standards can be displayed with relevance and confidence indicators to help users understand the recommendation.

### AI Explanation
The interface provides explanations for why a particular standard may be relevant to the procurement requirement.

### Standard Details
Users can view information related to individual standards, including their status and relevance.

### Related Standards
The platform provides an interface for identifying allied, related, testing, safety, and other potentially relevant standards.

### Tender Compliance Checker
Users can review tender requirements and identify potential compliance issues or missing references.

### Certification & Compliance
The interface provides a dedicated area for reviewing certification and compliance-related information.

### Procurement Reports
The platform includes an interface for generating procurement-related reports based on the selected standards and analysis.
